How this works

Hit Start, or tap an activity (Instagram, gaming…). As the clock runs you watch the money your time is worth burn away — every hour on a distraction is an hour you could have spent adding value to your life.

When you stop, you can turn that money around — commit it to 📈 invest, 🏦 save, or 💝 donate. Tap any logged session later to change its choice. Your Reports add it all up.

Why can't it detect apps automatically? A web app can't see what other apps you open — iOS walls apps off for privacy. So you tap to track (or wire up an iOS Shortcut — see the README). A native app could use Apple's Screen Time, but that needs a Mac to build.

Set your income, currency, nudges, goals and penalty rates in Settings. Everything stays on your device.
